# Collateral and Secured Lending Policy

## 1. Purpose and Scope

This policy sets the standards for accepting, valuing, perfecting and monitoring collateral that secures consumer credit at Meridian Bank, including secured cards, auto loans, secured personal loans and home equity lines.

## 2. Eligible Collateral and Loan-to-Value Limits

### 2.1 Maximum Loan-to-Value

Loan-to-value (LTV) is the credit amount divided by the collateral value after haircuts. The following maximum LTVs apply at origination:

| Collateral type | Valuation basis | Maximum LTV |
|---|---|---|
| Cash deposit or certificate of deposit held at Meridian Bank | Balance | 100% |
| New vehicle (up to 1 year old) | Invoice price | 90% |
| Used vehicle (1 to 8 years old) | Pricing guide value | 80% |
| Residential real estate, first lien | Independent appraisal | 80% |
| Residential real estate, second lien (home equity) | Independent appraisal | 70% combined LTV |
| Listed equities and exchange-traded funds | Market value | 50% |

Vehicles older than 8 years, cryptocurrencies, art, jewelry and unlisted shares are not eligible collateral.

### 2.2 Haircuts for Concentrated Securities

When a single security represents more than 40% of a pledged securities portfolio, an additional 20% haircut is applied to that position before calculating LTV.

## 3. Valuation

### 3.1 Appraisals

Real estate appraisals must be performed by an independent appraiser from the approved panel, must be no older than 6 months at origination, and must not be ordered by the loan officer who originates the loan.

### 3.2 Revaluation Frequency

- Real estate: every 36 months, and immediately when the loan becomes 90 or more days past due.
- Vehicles: annually, using the approved pricing guide.
- Securities: daily, mark-to-market.

### 3.3 Margin Calls on Securities

If the LTV of a securities-backed loan rises above 65%, the customer must be asked to restore the LTV to 50% within 5 business days, by adding collateral or repaying principal. If the LTV reaches 75%, the bank may sell pledged securities without further notice, as allowed by the loan agreement.

## 4. Perfection and Documentation

The lien on collateral must be perfected before funds are disbursed, except for vehicles purchased through an approved dealer, where the lien must be recorded within 30 days. The Collateral Operations team keeps the original lien documents and a digital copy in the document management system.

## 5. Secured Card Deposits

Deposits pledged for the Meridian Secured Card (see MB-POL-001 section 4.2) are held in a blocked savings account that pays the standard savings rate. The deposit is released 60 days after the card account is closed with a zero balance, or when the account graduates to an unsecured card.

## 6. Collateral Exceptions

Any LTV above the limits in section 2.1, and any collateral type not listed as eligible, requires approval by the Collateral Committee in addition to the credit approval required under MB-POL-005. The Collateral Committee meets weekly and is chaired by the Head of Retail Credit.

## 7. Insurance

Vehicles and real estate used as collateral must be insured for at least the outstanding loan amount, with Meridian Bank named as loss payee. Proof of insurance is verified at origination and annually.
