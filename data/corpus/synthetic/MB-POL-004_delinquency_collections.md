# Delinquency Management and Collections Policy

## 1. Purpose and Scope

This policy defines how Meridian Bank classifies delinquent consumer accounts, how and when customers are contacted, which hardship options may be offered, and when accounts are placed on non-accrual and charged off. It applies to credit cards, personal loans and secured consumer loans.

## 2. Delinquency Buckets

An account is delinquent when the minimum payment has not been received by the due date. Days past due (DPD) are counted from the first missed due date.

| Bucket | Days past due | Owner |
|---|---|---|
| Early stage | 1 to 29 | Customer Care (reminders) |
| Bucket 1 | 30 to 59 | Collections, early stage team |
| Bucket 2 | 60 to 89 | Collections, early stage team |
| Bucket 3 | 90 to 119 | Collections, late stage team |
| Bucket 4 | 120 to 179 | Recovery team |

## 3. Customer Contact Standards

### 3.1 First Contact

The first reminder (SMS, email or push notification) must be sent no later than day 5 past due. A first phone contact attempt must be made by day 15 past due.

### 3.2 Contact Limits

- Calls may only be placed between 8:00 a.m. and 9:00 p.m. in the customer's local time.
- No more than 7 call attempts within any rolling 7-day period per account.
- Once a conversation has taken place, no further call for 7 days unless the customer asks for one.
- Collectors must never contact the customer's employer or relatives about the debt, except to locate the customer when all contact data has failed, and never disclosing the debt.

### 3.3 Vulnerable Customers

When a customer discloses serious illness, bereavement or another vulnerability, the collector must flag the account and transfer it to the Specialist Support team, which can pause collection activity for up to 60 days.

## 4. Hardship and Forbearance

### 4.1 Hardship Program

Customers with a documented temporary financial hardship (job loss, illness, natural disaster) may enroll in the Hardship Program, which can reduce the required monthly payment by up to 50% for up to 12 months. Interest on credit cards is reduced to 9.99% APR during enrollment.

### 4.2 Eligibility Limits

A customer may enroll in the Hardship Program no more than 2 times in any 5-year period. Accounts more than 150 days past due are not eligible.

### 4.3 Re-aging

An account may be re-aged (brought current) only after the customer makes 3 consecutive full monthly payments under a new agreement. An account may be re-aged no more than once in 12 months and no more than twice in 5 years.

## 5. Non-Accrual and Charge-Off

- Interest and fees stop accruing (non-accrual status) when an account reaches 90 days past due.
- Credit card balances are charged off at 180 days past due.
- Unsecured personal loans are charged off at 120 days past due.
- Secured loans are written down to collateral value, net of selling costs, at 120 days past due; the remainder is charged off.
- Accounts of customers who file for bankruptcy are charged off within 60 days of notification, unless the loan is secured and the collateral covers the balance.

## 6. Settlements

Collectors may offer a settlement of up to 30% off the outstanding balance on accounts 90 or more days past due. Settlement discounts between 30% and 50% require approval from a Collections Manager. Discounts above 50% require approval from the Head of Collections and Recovery.

## 7. Third-Party Agencies

Accounts may be placed with an external collection agency only after charge-off. Agencies must follow the contact standards in section 3 and are audited at least annually.
