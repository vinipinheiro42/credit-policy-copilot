# Credit Card Underwriting Policy

## 1. Purpose and Scope

This policy sets the minimum standards Meridian Bank applies when approving new consumer credit card accounts and when assigning initial credit limits. It applies to all unsecured and secured consumer card products originated through branches, the mobile app, the website and approved partners. Commercial and corporate cards are out of scope and are governed by the Commercial Card Policy.

Credit analysts must apply this policy together with the Credit Policy Exceptions and Approval Authority Policy (MB-POL-005), the KYC and AML Customer Due Diligence Policy (MB-POL-006) and the Customer Data Privacy Policy (MB-POL-007).

## 2. Eligibility Requirements

### 2.1 Applicant Eligibility

An applicant is eligible for a consumer credit card only if all of the following are true:

- The applicant is at least 18 years old.
- The applicant has completed Customer Identification Program (CIP) checks under MB-POL-006 with a "pass" outcome.
- The applicant is not on any sanctions list and has no open fraud flag.
- The applicant has no account with Meridian Bank that was charged off in the last 36 months.
- The applicant has no bankruptcy discharged in the last 24 months.

Eligibility requirements in this section cannot be waived through the exceptions process.

### 2.2 Credit Bureau Requirements

A credit bureau report no older than 30 days is required for every application. The minimum bureau score for an unsecured card is 620. Applicants with a bureau score between 580 and 619 may only be offered the Meridian Secured Card. Applicants with a bureau score below 580 must be declined for all card products.

Applicants with no bureau score ("thin file", fewer than two tradelines or less than 6 months of credit history) may be offered the Meridian Starter Card under section 4.3.

## 3. Risk Grading

### 3.1 Meridian Risk Score

Every application receives a Meridian Risk Score (MRS) from the approved application scorecard. The MRS ranges from 300 to 900. The MRS is mapped to a risk grade as follows:

| Risk grade | MRS range | Card decision |
|---|---|---|
| A | 760 to 900 | Eligible for all card products |
| B | 700 to 759 | Eligible for all card products |
| C | 640 to 699 | Eligible for standard cards only (no premium cards) |
| D | 580 to 639 | Eligible for the Meridian Secured Card only |
| E | 300 to 579 | Decline |

The same risk grade table is used by the Personal Loan Limits and Pricing Policy (MB-POL-002).

### 3.2 Affordability

The applicant's debt-to-income ratio (DTI), including the minimum payment on the proposed card calculated as 3% of the proposed limit, must not exceed 45%. Monthly debt payments are taken from the bureau report plus any declared obligations. Income is the gross monthly income stated by the applicant or verified under section 5.

## 4. Credit Limit Assignment

### 4.1 Limit Formula

The initial credit limit is the lower of (a) the risk grade cap and (b) three times the applicant's verified net monthly income, rounded down to the nearest 100.

| Risk grade | Maximum initial limit (USD) |
|---|---|
| A | 25,000 |
| B | 15,000 |
| C | 8,000 |
| D | 3,000 (secured card only) |

### 4.2 Secured Card

For the Meridian Secured Card the credit limit equals the cash deposit pledged by the customer, between USD 300 and USD 3,000. The deposit is held in a blocked savings account as described in the Collateral and Secured Lending Policy (MB-POL-003). After 12 consecutive on-time payments the account is reviewed for graduation to an unsecured card.

### 4.3 Starter Card for Thin-File Applicants

Thin-file applicants with a verified income may receive a Meridian Starter Card with a limit between USD 500 and USD 1,500. No credit limit increase is allowed during the first 6 months on book.

### 4.4 Credit Limit Increases

Customers may receive a credit limit increase no more than once every 6 months, and only if the account has had no payment 30 or more days past due in the last 12 months. A new bureau report is required for any increase above USD 2,000.

## 5. Income Verification

Income must be verified with documentary evidence when the requested or assigned limit exceeds USD 5,000, or when the stated income is more than twice the median income for the applicant's declared occupation. Acceptable evidence includes the two most recent pay slips, the most recent tax return, or 3 months of bank statements. Verification documents are Confidential data under MB-POL-007.

## 6. Adverse Action

When an application is declined or approved with a lower limit than requested, the applicant must receive an adverse action notice within 30 days that lists the principal reasons for the decision. Reasons must be derived from the scorecard reason codes and must never reference prohibited characteristics listed in MB-POL-007.

## 7. Monitoring

Retail Credit Risk reports approval rate, average assigned limit, early delinquency (30+ days past due within 6 months on book) and exception rate by risk grade to the Retail Credit Committee every month.
