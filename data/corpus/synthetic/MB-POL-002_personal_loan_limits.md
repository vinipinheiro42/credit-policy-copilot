# Personal Loan Limits and Pricing Policy

## 1. Purpose and Scope

This policy defines the amounts, terms and interest rate bands for unsecured consumer personal loans at Meridian Bank. Secured personal loans follow this policy for amounts and terms and the Collateral and Secured Lending Policy (MB-POL-003) for collateral requirements.

## 2. Product Parameters

### 2.1 Amounts and Terms

- Minimum loan amount: USD 1,000.
- Maximum loan amount: USD 50,000, subject to the risk grade caps in section 3.
- Standard terms: 12 to 60 months.
- Terms of 61 to 84 months are only available for risk grade A applicants borrowing more than USD 25,000.

### 2.2 Aggregate Unsecured Exposure

The total unsecured exposure of a customer across all Meridian Bank products (personal loans plus card limits plus overdraft limits) must not exceed USD 75,000. Any approval above this amount requires Credit Committee approval under MB-POL-005.

## 3. Limits by Risk Grade

Risk grades use the Meridian Risk Score table defined in MB-POL-001 section 3.1.

| Risk grade | Maximum loan amount (USD) | Maximum DTI | APR band |
|---|---|---|---|
| A | 50,000 | 45% | 7.99% to 11.99% |
| B | 35,000 | 45% | 12.00% to 16.99% |
| C | 20,000 | 40% | 17.00% to 23.99% |
| D | 7,500 | 35% | 24.00% to 29.99% |
| E | Not eligible | Not applicable | Not applicable |

The DTI calculation includes the new loan installment. DTI limits may be exceeded by at most 5 percentage points through an approved exception under MB-POL-005.

## 4. Pricing

### 4.1 Rate Setting

The APR offered must fall inside the band for the applicant's risk grade. Within the band, pricing considers term, amount and relationship. Customers with an active direct deposit to a Meridian checking account for at least 6 months receive a 0.50 percentage point relationship discount, provided the final APR stays within the band.

### 4.2 Fees

The origination fee is 1% of the loan amount, capped at USD 500. No prepayment penalty is charged on any personal loan. The late fee is USD 25 when a payment is more than 10 days late.

## 5. Verification Requirements

Income verification under MB-POL-001 section 5 is mandatory for every personal loan above USD 10,000 and for every loan to a risk grade C or D applicant, regardless of amount.

## 6. Debt Consolidation Loans

For loans whose purpose is debt consolidation, at least 70% of the proceeds must be disbursed directly to the creditors being paid off. The DTI is calculated after removing the payments of the debts being consolidated.

## 7. Cooling-Off Period

Customers may cancel a personal loan without fees within 14 calendar days of disbursement by returning the principal. Interest accrued for the days the funds were held is charged.
