# Retail Credit Glossary and Analyst FAQ

## 1. Glossary

### 1.1 Debt-to-Income Ratio (DTI)

Total monthly debt payments, including the proposed new obligation, divided by gross monthly income. Limits are set in MB-POL-001 (cards) and MB-POL-002 (personal loans).

### 1.2 Loan-to-Value (LTV)

Credit amount divided by the collateral value after haircuts. Limits are set in MB-POL-003.

### 1.3 Days Past Due (DPD)

Number of days since the first missed payment due date. Delinquency buckets are defined in MB-POL-004.

### 1.4 Total Exposure

The sum of all limits and outstanding balances a customer holds at Meridian Bank after the proposed credit. It determines the required approval level under MB-POL-005.

## 2. Analyst FAQ

### 2.1 Can I approve an exception myself if I am a Senior Credit Analyst?

No. Every exception requires approval one level above the normal approval level, with a minimum of Level 2 (Credit Manager). See MB-POL-005 section 3.3.

### 2.2 The CIP result is "refer". Can I continue the credit analysis?

You may prepare the analysis, but you cannot approve the application. Only Financial Crime Compliance can resolve a "refer" outcome (MB-POL-006 section 2.3).

### 2.3 Can I paste an applicant's pay slip into a public chatbot to summarize it?

No. Pay slips are Confidential data and must never be entered into external AI tools (MB-POL-007 section 5).

### 2.4 When does a credit card get charged off?

At 180 days past due. Unsecured personal loans are charged off at 120 days past due (MB-POL-004 section 5).
