# Credit Policy Exceptions and Approval Authority Policy

## 1. Purpose and Scope

This policy defines who may approve consumer credit at Meridian Bank, how much they may approve, and how deviations from credit policy ("exceptions") are requested, approved, documented and monitored.

## 2. Delegated Approval Authority

### 2.1 Authority Matrix

Approval authority is based on the customer's total exposure after the new credit, including all existing Meridian Bank limits and balances.

| Authority level | Role | Maximum total exposure (USD) |
|---|---|---|
| Level 1 | Senior Credit Analyst | 10,000 |
| Level 2 | Credit Manager | 50,000 |
| Level 3 | Head of Retail Credit | 150,000 |
| Committee | Retail Credit Committee | Above 150,000 |

Credit Analysts below the Senior level have no individual approval authority; their recommendations must be approved by a Level 1 or higher approver.

### 2.2 Four-Eyes Principle

The person who prepares a credit recommendation may not approve it. Approvers cannot approve credit for themselves, relatives or companies in which they hold an interest.

## 3. Policy Exceptions

### 3.1 Definition

An exception is any approval that does not meet a requirement of MB-POL-001, MB-POL-002 or MB-POL-003, for example a DTI above the limit, a loan amount above the risk grade cap, or an LTV above the maximum.

### 3.2 Non-Waivable Requirements

The following requirements can never be waived through the exceptions process:

- Minimum age and legal capacity.
- KYC, CIP, sanctions screening and AML requirements under MB-POL-006.
- Open fraud flags.
- Prohibited-characteristic rules under MB-POL-007.
- The eligibility requirements in MB-POL-001 section 2.1.

### 3.3 Exception Limits and Required Approver

Every exception requires approval one authority level above the level that would normally approve the exposure, with a minimum of Level 2. Additional limits apply:

- DTI may be exceeded by at most 5 percentage points.
- Loan amounts may exceed the risk grade cap by at most 20%.
- No more than one exception may be granted on a single application; applications that need two or more exceptions must go to the Retail Credit Committee.
- LTV exceptions also require Collateral Committee approval under MB-POL-003 section 6.

### 3.4 Required Justification

The exception request must document compensating factors, such as significant liquid assets, long relationship tenure (more than 5 years), or a strong payment history with no 30-day delinquency in 24 months.

## 4. Exception Register

All approved exceptions must be recorded in the Exception Register within 2 business days of approval, including the policy clause waived, the compensating factors and the approver's name.

## 5. Monitoring and Escalation

The portfolio exception rate is the number of approved applications with an exception divided by all approved applications in the month. Retail Credit Risk reports it monthly.

- If the monthly exception rate exceeds 5%, the Head of Retail Credit must present a remediation plan to the Retail Credit Committee.
- If it exceeds 8% in any month, or 5% for three consecutive months, the matter is escalated to the Chief Risk Officer.

Accounts approved with an exception are tracked separately for early delinquency for 12 months.
